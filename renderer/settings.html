Troubleshooting

Reload client
Reloads the Showdown view. Any battle in progress will reconnect.
Logs
Attach these when reporting a bug.
Sign out of Showdown
Clears saved Showdown data, including your sign-in, local teams and client preferences. Replay notes are kept.
Reset launcher data
Deletes launcher settings, preferences and replay notes. Your Showdown sign-in and teams are kept. This cannot be undone.
